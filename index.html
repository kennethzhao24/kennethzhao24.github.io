Brief Bio

I am a final-year PhD candidate at the University of Central Florida, advised by Prof. Jun Wang. I received my M.Sc from Georgia Tech. I have also worked as a student researcher at Microsoft Research and Google AI Research.

My research interests lie in the intersections of machine learning, computer architecture, and operating systems, featured in ISCA, MLSys, ICLR, AAAI, etc, and recognized with ASONAM Best Paper Award and ML and System Rising Stars.

I am the core contributor and maintainer of FluxServe project, an open-source inference serving engine for diffusion language models.
