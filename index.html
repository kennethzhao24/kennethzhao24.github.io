Brief Bio

I am a 4th-year PhD candidate at the University of Central Florida, advised by Prof. Jun Wang. I received my M.Sc from Georgia Tech and my B.Eng degree from Wuhan University. I have also worked on as student researcher at Microsoft Research and Google. I am one of the cohorts for ML and System Rising Stars 2025.

My research interests lie in the intersections of machine learning and computer systems (MLSys). I build efficient and resilient machine learning systems for large-scale AI models.
